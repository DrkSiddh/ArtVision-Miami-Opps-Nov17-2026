Miami Art Week is November 30 through December 6, 2026. Art Basel itself opens to the public December 4–6 at the Convention Center, but the whole city is buzzing with satellite fairs, hotel galleries, projections, and pop-ups. Perfect time to exhibit.

First one that's still open and completely free: the One Love Art DAO flash call. They're looking for GTA VI / Vice City inspired digital art—horizontal, 3840x2160. Minted or unminted, new or old, just fun community vibes. Selected pieces get featured on the screens at the One Love Gallery inside the YVE Hotel Miami, which is an official GTA map site. Exhibition during the week, so you'll be in the mix with collectors and artists flooding the city.

Deadline is tomorrow—November 18, 11:59 PM ET. Super simple: just reply under their post on X with your piece. No long form, no fee. Free to apply, free to exhibit. They're partnering with the hotel and N3XTCULTURE. Search "OneLoveArtDAO GTA VI open call" or go straight to their latest post on X. This is pure Web3 energy—DAO, digital, community showcase. If you've got something that riffs on that neon Vice City energy, drop it tonight. You've got the vision; this is the stage.

Second, if you're thinking bigger and a small fee is worth the prestige: there's a call tied to CONTEXT Art Miami (the major satellite fair focused on emerging and mid-career artists, running Dec 1–6 right next to Art Miami). Deadline November 20. Application fee is $40. Why it's okay? Selected artists get an online exhibition through Artsy, a press release hitting 75,000+ contacts, collector and advisory exposure, 70% of net sales, and possible 12-month non-exclusive gallery representation. Up to 5 images. It's for artists worldwide working in any contemporary medium. Google "CONTEXT Art Miami artist selection Nova Gallery" or check the official guidelines via the linked form. Prestigious fair, real sales and visibility potential—the fee covers the selection process and that platform access. If your work is strong and you want that representation door open, it's worth a look.
